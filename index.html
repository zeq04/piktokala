// 01 — ABOUT

Every corner of campus,
through our lens.

Our Photography Club is the university's creative visual team — capturing and preserving memorable moments from campus events, cultural programs, seminars, sports, and celebrations.

We cover major university functions through event photography and videography, turning special moments into lasting memories while helping promote campus life on social media and in the university archives.

Alongside documentation, we give members real opportunities to grow — camera handling, editing, visual storytelling, and photojournalism — through workshops, photo walks, competitions, exhibitions, and hands-on event coverage.
